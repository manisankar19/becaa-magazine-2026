2.0 Midlife Crisis and its related Health Issues:

“Middle Age Crisis” or “Midlife Crisis” is probably not an unknown topic to most of you, especially for the readers of the Times of India. It is a period of emotional turbulence, self-reflection, and identity reassessment typically occurring between ages 40 and 50, often triggered by confronting one’s mortality or unfulfilled goals. It involves anxiety and restlessness, often leading to impulsive behavior, major lifestyle changes, or desire for youthfulness. Though it is not a diagnosable mental disorder yet it can cause severe emotional distress. As a result, people of this age group suffer from various medical disorders like hypertension (high blood Pressure), diabetes or hyperglycemia, anxiety, sleep issues, depression, irritability, and physical aches. Also, osteoarthritis (OA), which is considered a stress-related disorder, is very common in this age group. Additionally many of these people faces difficulty coping with changes.

4.0 Medicine Free Life:

Yes, I am now leading a medicine free life. Let me share how? Let us first understand the following steps:

Step 1: Break the myths:

Myths are those, which are not true but we believe as if they are. There are many myths, which doctors force us to believe.

The doctors are the experts in their own fields. Most of them do not know anything about fitness and wellness. Hence, believing the doctors are the biggest myths.

Hypertension is not a disease. BP medicines are meant for lowering the blood pressures at given point of time and hence, they need not taken liken lifelong. If somebody is serious, he/she can come out from this slavery easily.

One person suffering from Knee pain, joint pain, osteoarthritis etc. can walk/jog/run or climb up/climb down staircases, seat in Indian toilet and Vajrasana provided do certain muscle strengthening exercises.

Step 2: Change in Life Styles:

The best doctor to treat your midlife related syndromes is your own body. Hence, please allow it to treat you adequately.

Early to rise and early to bed make a person healthy and wise. Please wake up early, as early as 4o’clock before the sun rise and sleep as early as possible.

Do not take any food after sunset. If not possible, please finish your dinner by 8 PM

Eating less is always better.

Step 3: Make Exercises as your Newest Hobby:

Many of the people of ripen age group feels physical exercises are not for them. However, it is totally a wrong thinking. Let us examine with a little bit of elaboration.

No doctor had ever told me that running reduces Blood Pressure. Running regularly is an effective, drug-free way to lower high blood pressure (hypertension) by making the heart stronger, allowing it to pump more blood with less effort. While blood pressure rises initially but it drops afterward (sometimes for up to 24 hours) as blood vessels remain dilated.

Cycling can significantly improve knee joint pain, especially for those with osteoarthritis or recovering from minor injuries. Because it is a low-impact, non-weight-bearing exercise, your body weight is supported by the seat, which reduces stress and compression on the knee joints compared to running or walking.

Gym exercises like leg curls and leg extensions – both can reduce knee pain by strengthening the muscles that support and stabilize the joint. However, they must be done with proper form and appropriate weight, as incorrect technique.

Pranayama & meditation are the excellent methods to manage the stress. Pranayama increases lung capacity, clears phlegm, and improves cardiovascular health.

Backward Walking can be a panacea to prevent some of our old age problems.

Finally, learn to relax. Take a mini vacation whenever possible. According to some people, vacation is a leisure time affair but factually it can also be a health recovering remedy and prevention of many occupational diseases.
